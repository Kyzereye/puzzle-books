Two Steps forward – one step back.  That step back is just realigning to blast forward.

The road toward any goal is never a straight line.  Unforeseen bumps along the way can knock us off course, but so can a change of plans or a new desire.  Goals and dreams change as we grow and taking a step back every now and then is a healthy check in to where you are.  Don't be so ridged in reaching your goals that you don't look around to see what else is out there.  You may miss something that is greater then you ever imagined.
